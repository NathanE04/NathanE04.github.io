About Me

Hi, my name is Nathan Elliott. I'm a 1st year student in IMD at Algonquin College. I have previously graduated from the art stream of the Game Development program. I enjoy creating all kinds of digital art, whether it's drawing, 3D modeling or graphic design. Currently, I am learning HTML in Web Design Foundations.

Along with drawing, and creating 3D models in my spare time I play a lot of video games. I have two Siberian cats who are siblings named Cyber and Pixel.
